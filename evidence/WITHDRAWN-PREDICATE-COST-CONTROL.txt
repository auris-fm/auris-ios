WITHDRAWN: the "predicate cost" control in 979df22c6
Reviewer, e9d87bdf. The receipt is withdrawn, not amended: its central claim does not hold.

THE CLAIM THAT FAILED
  979df22c6 reported "20 unresolved settles => 0 acquisitions", with a red run producing 20.
  Both halves are vacuous. The counter was attached to `credentialAcquisitionForTesting`, a
  property NO PRODUCTION PATH CALLS, so zero invocations was guaranteed by the unused
  property rather than produced by the predicate under test. The red run then inserted a call
  to that property, which proves that an artificial call I wrote is counted - the opposite of
  proving that real acquisition would be intercepted.

  Green with the defect absent, red with a call I inserted, and in both directions the outcome
  was decided by the seam rather than by the code. A control whose answer is structured into
  its own fixture measures the fixture.

WHY IT LOOKED FINE
  I had a correct instinct - "that is an inspection, not a measurement" - and then built
  something that did not meet it. Naming the distinction is not the same as honouring it, and
  the review caught the gap between the two.

WHAT ACTUALLY HOLDS
  A source-inspection conclusion, and only that: the readiness readers the predicate consults
  are `ServerSettings.committedAccountIdentity()` (lock + in-memory), `SyncManager
  .isUserLoggedIn()` (a defaults read) and `ServerSettings.storedAccountIdentity()` (a Keychain
  read). All three are synchronous, network-free reads. The reviewed paths do not acquire
  tokens.
  What is NOT established: that the predicate performs no acquisition on paths I did not read.
  Cost-acquisition coverage on a real network path remains absent, and no control added here
  produced it.

RULE ADOPTED (cloud, 7dbe468f, correcting his own earlier formulation)
  A counter attached to a property nothing calls returns zero irrespective of the code under
  test, so an unexercised seam guarantees the zero it reports. The counter must sit on the path
  production actually takes, and the collaborators it replaces must remain the real ones
  wherever the measurement matters. "Invoked by nothing" makes a count vacuous, not meaningful.

SCOPE
  No simulator work. Per spec (f1c3c351, 339d9f21) this verification scope is closed: the
  installed build stays at bb72d3c4c and no further controls or test hooks are being added.
